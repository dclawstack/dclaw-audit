The platform

One system.
Audit start to report.

DClaw Audit is an AI-native evidence-to-finding operating system for internal audit teams. It compresses audit cycle time without adding headcount by wiring AI into every step — from drafting evidence requests to generating the final board report.

Built on the One Convergence design system and deployed on the DKube infrastructure philosophy: your data, your infrastructure, full control.

📋

Engagement & evidence management

Full CRUD for audit engagements, evidence requests (with version log), file uploads to MinIO, overdue tracking.

🤖

AI at every audit decision point

Draft evidence requests, brainstorm risks, structure findings, prioritize by risk, generate remediation plans, and write the final report — all from context.

🔬

Anomaly detection & continuous signals

Ingest transactions for rule + statistical anomaly flagging. Triage ERP, IAM, and cloud signals before fieldwork starts.

📊

Cross-engagement intelligence

Recurring control failures, owner responsiveness analytics, and cycle time benchmarks across all past engagements.
