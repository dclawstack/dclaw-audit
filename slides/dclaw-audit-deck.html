DClaw Audit 03 / 13
The problem

Internal audit teams run on email, spreadsheets, and hope.

01

Evidence chaos

Requests scattered across email threads, SharePoint folders, and sticky notes. No version tracking, no overdue visibility, no completeness check.

02

Finding lag

Exceptions surface weeks after fieldwork because drafting, structuring, and prioritising findings is manual and slow. Remediation never closes on time.

03

No intelligence layer

Recurring control failures go unnoticed because data lives per-engagement. Owner accountability is anecdotal. Cycle time is a guess.

DClaw Audit puts AI at every step — from drafting evidence requests to writing the final board report.
